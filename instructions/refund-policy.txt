# Refund Policy

## Auto-Approve Scenarios

Automatically approve refunds when ALL of these conditions are met:

1. **Low-value refunds**: Amount ≤ $100 with clear, legitimate reason
2. **Loyal customers**: Customers with >1 year history AND amount ≤ $300 AND legitimate reasons
3. **Legitimate reasons include**:
   - Defective product
   - Wrong item received
   - Item damaged in shipping
   - Product doesn't match description

## Request Human Approval

Escalate to human when ANY of these apply:

1. **Medium amounts**: $100-$500 for customers with <3 months history
2. **High amounts**: Any refund > $500
3. **Vague reasons**: Unclear or inconsistent explanation
4. **High frequency**: Customer has requested multiple refunds recently (>2 in past 30 days)
5. **Policy edge cases**: Situations not clearly covered by auto-approve or reject rules

## Auto-Reject Scenarios

Automatically reject refunds when:

1. **Non-refundable items**: Product is marked as final sale / non-refundable
2. **Expired timeframe**: Request is >90 days after purchase
3. **Fraud indicators**: Account is flagged for fraudulent activity or abuse
4. **Pattern of abuse**: Customer has history of excessive refund requests
5. **Clear policy violation**: Request explicitly violates stated refund policy

## Examples

### Auto-Approve Examples
- "Refund $75 for defective headphones, customer purchased 2 weeks ago"
- "Customer Jane Doe (member since 2022) requesting $250 refund for wrong size shoes"

### Request Approval Examples
- "$450 refund for customer who joined last month, reason: 'didn't like it'"
- "$850 refund for damaged laptop - exceeds auto-approve threshold"
- "Customer requesting 3rd refund this month for $120"

### Auto-Reject Examples
- "Refund request for item purchased 6 months ago (exceeds 90-day window)"
- "Request from account flagged for multiple chargeback disputes"
- "Refund for digital download marked as non-refundable"
